# GWW3 — Logo Animation / Teaser Video Prompts

Use these prompts with video generation tools (Google Veo, Runway ML, Pika, Sora, etc.)

---

## Prompt A: Logo Reveal (5-10 seconds)

> A dark navy void. A 3D globe slowly materializes from particles of light, rotating. Golden fracture lines crack across its surface like spreading fault lines, glowing with energy. The camera slowly pulls back as the cracks intensify. Military HUD elements fade in around the globe — radar circles, coordinate grids, data readouts flickering. The text "GAMES OF WORLD WAR 3" assembles letter by letter in metallic stencil font below the globe, each letter landing with a subtle impact. Final frame: complete logo, globe still slowly rotating. Cinematic lighting, dark atmosphere, no music. Style: Pentagon war room holographic display.

---

## Prompt B: Geopolitical Teaser (15-20 seconds)

> Opening shot: Earth from space, nighttime, city lights visible. Slow zoom toward Europe. As we get closer, golden lines appear tracing country borders — glowing fault lines of geopolitical tension. Cut to: a radar screen view from above, showing red conflict hotspots pulsing in the Middle East, Ukraine, Africa. Cut to: a military command center screen showing scrolling data — GDP numbers, military deployments, alliance networks as connected graphs. Cut to: the 3D globe from the logo, cracking open with golden light. Text appears: "195 Nations. Real Data. Your Strategy." Beat. Then: "GAMES OF WORLD WAR 3" in metallic stencil font. Final text: "Coming 2026." Dark cinematic atmosphere throughout. Camera movements smooth and deliberate. No people, only data, maps, and the globe.

---

## Prompt C: Data-Driven Montage (10-15 seconds)

> Fast-paced montage of data visualizations: a world map with trade flow arcs lighting up between nations, military unit icons moving across borders, economic charts rising and crashing, alliance network diagrams connecting and breaking apart. Each visualization uses the dark navy + command gold palette. Between cuts, brief flashes of the fractured globe. The pace accelerates. Final cut: everything freezes. The globe materializes in the center. "GAMES OF WORLD WAR 3" slams into frame. Tagline fades in: "Every nation. Every decision. Every consequence." Style: high-tech data visualization meets cinematic trailer.
